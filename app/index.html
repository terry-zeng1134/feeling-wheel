Where the words come from

Structure follows Gloria Willcox's Feeling Wheel (Transactional Analysis Journal, 1982): 6 core feelings, 36, then 72. A fourth ring is marked ext. and cites Shaver et al. (1987) or Cowen & Keltner (2017). Definitions are authored, not from those sources. The 72-word tree is a reconstruction and has not been verified word-for-word against the 1982 paper.
